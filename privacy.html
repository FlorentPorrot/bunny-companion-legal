Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 16, 2026
Updates: Adapted for Bunny Care Companion, including Health Tracking logs, Care Reminders, and Rabbit Profile management.

This privacy policy applies to the Bunny Care Companion app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Florent Porrot (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information to help you manage your rabbit's care. This information includes:

  • Rabbit Profiles: Names, breeds, birthdays, and weight of your rabbits to calculate age and track health trends.
  • Health Logs: Data regarding your rabbit's appetite, poop status (e.g., healthy, soft, stringy), energy levels, and medical notes.
  • Care Checklist: History of completed tasks like feeding, cleaning, and grooming.
  • Device Information: Basic technical data like OS version and device model for bug tracking.
  • User Accounts: Your email address is used to securely sync your rabbit's data across multiple devices.

The Application does not gather precise location data (GPS) from your mobile device.

2. Third-Party Services

Aggregated or necessary data is transmitted to secure external services to provide core features and improve app stability. We use:

2.1 Database & Auth (Supabase)

We use Supabase to securely store and sync your rabbit profiles, health logs, and account information.

  • Supabase Privacy Policy

2.2 Payments (RevenueCat)

We use RevenueCat to manage "Bunny Care Plus" subscriptions and premium features.

  • Data Collected: An anonymous App User ID and purchase history to verify your subscription status.
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

3. Care Reminders & Notifications

The Application allows you to schedule reminders for tasks like nail trims, vet visits, or daily feedings.

  • These notifications are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Your specific care schedules are not shared with external marketing platforms.
  • You can manage or disable these at any time in the app settings.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active to provide the synchronization service.

Right to Delete: You have the right to delete your account and all associated rabbit data at any time. You can perform this action directly within the application by navigating to Profile > Account > Delete Account. Alternatively, you may contact us at bunny-companion@proton.me to request manual deletion.

5. Children

The Application is a care tool for pet owners. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at bunny-companion@proton.me for immediate deletion.

6. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. We recommend checking this page regularly for updates. Continued use of the app signifies your acceptance of any changes.
